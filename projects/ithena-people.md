# iThena People (Onboarding Portal)

**Type:** Internal HR application (employee onboarding)
**Stack:** Claude Code, HTML, Git
**Role:** Co-builder (UI, email templates, sign-off workflow)

## Problem
Onboarding new employees involved scattered emails and manual approvals, with no single place to track where each hire was in the process.

## What I built
- Turned the business and product requirements (BRD and PRD) into a working application with a teammate, starting from an HTML mockup built with Claude Code.
- Owned the UI and the automated onboarding email templates (layout, branding and copy).
- Built a multi-level sign-off workflow that routes each step from the employee up to HR for final review.
- After a leadership review, reworked the experience to be simpler for first-time users.

## What I learned
- Requirements documents are a starting point; the first leadership demo is where the real requirements show up.
- Simple flows beat feature-rich ones for people who only use a tool once.

## Outcome
A working onboarding portal with automated emails and a clear approval chain.
